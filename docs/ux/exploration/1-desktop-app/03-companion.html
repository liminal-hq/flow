1

The pill. A 30-pt always-on-top sliver: state dot, stack-depth dots (thread ▸ branch), title, time in focus. It answers “what am I supposed to be doing?” without a single click — the desktop equivalent of a shell prompt segment.

2

Hover to act. The pill blooms into its four verbs — done, park, back, open. Every state change here is just a CLI command under the hood, written to the shared SQLite store; the TUI in your terminal sees it within 250 ms.

3

Tray as resume menu. The tray icon carries the state colour. Its flyout shows the live stack plus the three most recently paused/parked streams — one click on wear os sync runs the same logic as r in the TUI: activate, auto-pause the rest.

4

The welcome-back card. After system idle/unlock (Tauri can watch both), Flow doesn’t nag — it offers. Your last note is the memory jog; one key resumes, one key pauses honestly. Drift becomes data instead of guilt.
