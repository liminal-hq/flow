1

Blocks are recorded, never planned. A block appears when you start a focus and grows while you stay. The day column is append-only history in calendar clothing — familiar to read, impossible to fall behind on, because there is nothing to fill in.

2

Gaps are kept, labelled, and neutral. Time away is drawn as honest whitespace — "nothing recorded, nothing invented." No app-filling, no shame red. The record stays trustworthy precisely because it admits what it doesn't know.

3

Interruptions read as geology. The morning shows AIDX split by a 25-minute Wear OS interlude — three strata instead of one lie about "3 hours of AIDX." Sub-tasks (the live green block) carry their parentage in the subtitle, so the story stays legible.

4

The right panel answers three questions. Now (what's running), Selected block (what was I thinking then — click any block to replay its notes), and Not-yet-today (the queue, in order). Fixed sections, no tabs, no rearranging.

5

Notes are pinned to time × block. Every note lands on the block that was running when you wrote it. Clicking 13:05–13:41 shows exactly the two thoughts from that half hour — "what was I thinking before lunch?" becomes a click, not an archaeology dig.

6

Week view is the same data, folded. Seven day-columns side by side, blocks coloured by focus. Because it's all recorded, the week view doubles as a truthful timesheet — export it, or just stop guessing on Friday.
